Submit a zine

Bring us the finished thing.

Fill this out, choose your files, and we’ll prepare a submission email with everything the distro needs to review your work.

Before you send

The webpage validates your files locally. It does not upload them. Your email app will open a draft; attach the two selected files before sending.

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

Make the handoff easy.

  1. We review the zine and its permissions.
  2. We may reply with a question or a request for a better file.
  3. If accepted, we publish it with your chosen credit and the distribution permissions you approved.
  4. You can ask us to update or remove it later.

For now: the email draft uses the existing Samizdat support address. We’ll replace it with a dedicated submissions address before a public launch.

The distro promise

More than a showcase.

A finished zine is meant to move.

01 / CREDIT

Name the maker.

Every published zine will carry the creator’s chosen credit and a link or note they approve.

02 / ACCESS

Make it usable.

Where permission allows, entries will include a downloadable file and practical notes for printing, cutting, and folding.

03 / HANDOFF

Help it travel.

Visitors should be encouraged to print a copy, give it to someone, teach from it, or make another copy with permission.

Make the zine. We’ll help it travel.
