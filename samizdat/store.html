The full-bleed trap: none of these should be advertised as automatic two-sided full-bleed printers. Consumer drivers normally disable borderless mode while duplexing. Expect a manual two-pass workflow and alignment tests—or print oversized and trim. Dye inks also are not the same as archival pigment inks.

Retailer reality check

Convenience is not a certification.

Blick has the broadest useful art-supply affiliate catalog, but its March 2026 disclosure says it does not use third parties for formal supply-chain risk assessments or audits and does not require material-level supplier certification. Michaels reports third-party social-accountability audits, though its catalog did not produce the strongest SKU in every category.

For printers, Abt is the most attractive multi-brand affiliate retailer we found: family-owned, with repair, solar, and an on-site recycling operation—claims that are still primarily self-reported. B&H has a useful catalog but is not our ethical default because of documented past labor settlements.
