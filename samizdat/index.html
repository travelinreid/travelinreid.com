Why make a zine now?

The physical world could use more brave little objects.

Let others feed the feeds. Let them scroll to eternity. Let the platforms filter, throttle, ban, and bury till the churn is one froth.

We will fold an idea into a hand-to-hand lifeboat. A paper vessel for a bold idea, a small kindness, a field guide, a warning, a joke, a lesson, or a love letter.

Samizdat is not another platform. It helps you make a real thing and put it out into the world.
